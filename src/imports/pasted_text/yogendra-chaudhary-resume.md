# My Design Process

I don't follow a rigid process for every project. I adapt my approach based on the problem, users, timeline, and project requirements.

### 01 — Understand

I start by understanding the problem, requirements, target users, and business context.

### 02 — Research

I gather information about users, existing solutions, competitors, and potential pain points.

### 03 — Define

I organize the findings and identify the key problems that the product needs to solve.

### 04 — Explore

I explore different ideas through user flows, information architecture, sketches, and wireframes.

### 05 — Design

I transform the strongest ideas into high-fidelity interfaces while focusing on hierarchy, usability, consistency, and visual design.

### 06 — Prototype

I connect screens and interactions in Figma to create a realistic prototype and test the overall flow.

### 07 — Iterate

Design is never truly finished. I review feedback, identify problems, and refine the experience.
